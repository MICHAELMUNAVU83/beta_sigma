Who We Are

BeCorp partners with exceptional people and ideas to build industries that solve real-world challenges. Our portfolio spans entertainment, agriculture, real estate and professional services, with each company operating independently while benefiting from the Group’s strategic leadership, governance and shared capabilities.

Our vision

Building industries that shape Africa’s future — creating lasting economic and social impact through innovation, disciplined execution and long-term partnerships.

Our approach

We believe successful industries are built on strong governance, technology, operational excellence and strategic partnerships. Through shared expertise and disciplined execution, we enable each subsidiary to scale sustainably while creating long-term value for stakeholders.

  1. 01 Strong governance across every business in the Group
  2. 02 Technology-driven operations and operational excellence
  3. 03 Strategic partnerships that unlock scale
  4. 04 Long-term value creation for all stakeholders

A diversified group of companies

Tukutane Entertainment

An experience-led entertainment company producing large-scale concerts, festivals and premium live experiences across East Africa — with 50,000+ attendees, $1.5M+ invested in arts and culture, and $2.0M+ in delivered sponsor and partner value.

Chasing Sun Agriculture

A technology-enabled agribusiness modernising agricultural production, commodity sourcing and supply chains across East Africa — currently operating 19 greenhouses in Naivasha with 2MT+ in weekly production.

SandCorp

A real estate development company delivering innovative residential, commercial and mixed-use developments across Kenya, backed by a live pipeline of active projects.

Sichangi Law Alliance (SLA)

A modern law firm providing legal advisory, dispute resolution and corporate legal services to industries and institutions.
