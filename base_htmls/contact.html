Frequently asked questions

How BeCorp builds, scales and manages high-growth industries across strategic sectors — and how investors, corporates, entrepreneurs and strategic partners can work with us.

01

What does BeCorp do?

BeCorp is a diversified investment and operating company that builds, scales and manages high-growth industries across strategic sectors. Through innovation, disciplined execution and long-term partnerships, we develop industries that create lasting economic and social impact across Africa.

02

Which companies are in the Group?

The portfolio spans Tukutane Entertainment, Chasing Sun Agriculture, SandCorp and Sichangi Law Alliance (SLA) — covering entertainment, agriculture, real estate and professional services.

03

How does the Group work with each company?

Each company operates independently while benefiting from the Group’s strategic leadership, governance and shared capabilities. Through shared expertise and disciplined execution, we enable each subsidiary to scale sustainably while creating long-term value for stakeholders.

04

Are you open for partnership?

Yes. We’re always looking to collaborate with investors, corporates, entrepreneurs and strategic partners who share our vision of building scalable industries across Africa.

BeCorp builds, scales and manages high-growth industries across entertainment, agriculture, real estate and professional services.
